CNG CASCADE TYPE IV

Introduction

Type-4 CNG Cascade Systems from Yash Engineering Company utilize advanced composite cylinders designed to achieve maximum CNG storage capacity with significantly reduced weight. These ultra-lightweight cylinders are ideal for mobile cascade applications, where minimizing vehicle weight and maximizing payload efficiency are critical.

Type-4 cylinders are non-corrosive, high-performance, and technologically advanced, making them a modern alternative to traditional steel cascades. While Type-4 cascades are currently more expensive, their cost is steadily decreasing, positioning them as a future-ready solution for next-generation CNG infrastructure.

Key Advantages of Type-IV Cascades

  • Ultra-lightweight construction
  • Higher CNG storage capacity
  • Excellent corrosion resistance
  • Improved fuel transport efficiency
  • Longer operational life with minimal maintenance

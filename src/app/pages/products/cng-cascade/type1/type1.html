CNG CASCADE TYPE I

Introduction

At Yash Engineering Company, we design, manufacture, and supply high-quality CNG Cascade Systems for the safe storage and transportation of Compressed Natural Gas (CNG). Our cascade systems are engineered for use in CNG mother stations, daughter booster stations, mobile gas transportation units, and industrial fuel supply applications.

A CNG Cascade System consists of a group of high-pressure cylinders interconnected through high-grade stainless steel tubing and mounted on a robust structural steel frame, forming a single gas storage unit. Each cylinder is positioned at a defined distance to ensure operational safety, uniform gas flow, and ease of maintenance.

All CNG cascades manufactured by Yash Engineering Company comply with PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization) guidelines, ensuring strict adherence to safety and regulatory standards.

Type-1 CNG Cascade Systems

Type-1 CNG Cascade Systems offered by Yash Engineering Company are manufactured using seamless steel cylinders, designed and tested for high-pressure gas storage.

Type-1 cascades are widely used for both stationary installations and mobile transportation of CNG, particularly for transferring gas from mother stations to daughter booster stations. Although they are heavier compared to composite cylinders, their cost-effectiveness, durability, and proven field performance make them the most commonly used cascade systems in India.

Key Advantages of Type-I Cascades

  • Economical and reliable solution
  • High mechanical strength and durability
  • Suitable for stationary and mobile applications
  • Easy maintenance and long service life
  • Widely accepted by regulatory authorities
